About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for writing down your ideas fast. It helps you keep all your notes in one place so nothing gets forgotten.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note in the box.
  2. Click the Add Note button.
  3. See your note appear in the list.

Features

  • Add notes quickly
  • See all your notes in one list
  • See how many notes you have

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note
Tab Move to the next field
